Hero Bingo

Tick off challenges on a 5x5 card. Five in a row, column or diagonal is a bingo.

Connecting…
Round won

Each player has a separate card. Tap a square when you complete it, tap again to undo.

Ticking as

One card for the whole group. Pick who you are, then tap a square you completed. Several people can tick the same square: each tap adds or removes only you, and every initial stays on it.

    New cards are drawn from the enabled challenges. Cards already on the table keep their squares until you draw again.

    Cards and challenges are shared live: everyone who opens this page sees the same cards. Your tab and player choice stay on your device.
